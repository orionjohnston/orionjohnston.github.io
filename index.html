Gabriel Johnston

Geophysicist & Organist

I study how Earth’s deep mantle moves and mixes, by tracking particles through time-reversed convection models. I am also an organist and choirmaster, with improvisation at the center of my playing.

About

The Scientist

I completed my PhD in Geology at the University of Florida in 2026, advised by Professor Alessandro Forte and working in conjunction with the Institut de Physique du Globe de Paris. I am now a Courtesy Assistant Professor in UF’s Department of Geological Sciences and teach Earth Sciences at St. Petersburg College.

My research combines mantle geodynamics with isotope geochemistry, using particle tracking in convection models constrained by seismic tomography to ask where hotspot lavas come from and how well the mantle is mixed.

Research & publications

The Musician

I am Organist & Choirmaster at Calvary Episcopal Church in Indian Rocks Beach, Florida, where I direct the adult choir and bell choir and oversee the rebuilding of the music program after Hurricane Helene. I also sing bass with the Master Chorale of Tampa Bay.

I have studied improvisation with Sophie-Véronique Cauchefer-Choplin, Jeffrey Brillhart, Frédéric Blanc, Samuel Liégeon, David Cassan, and Nicola Canzano, and received the 2025 Raymond Glover Grant from the Association of Anglican Musicians.
